Family operations

Bookings, inclusions & mobility notes

These are the details that make the plan actually work on the ground, not just look good on a schedule.

🏨 Bangkok — Prince Palace

  • Initial Bangkok stay: 2 nights; return stay: 1 night.
  • Mid-trip large suitcase storage is confirmed free.
  • Late check-in after the Krabi return is confirmed.
  • Refundable security deposit: THB 1,000 per room per stay; not treated as a trip expense.
  • Hotel sits above Bobae Market; allow extra time for pickups in the crowded surrounding streets.

🏠 Krabi — Otter House

  • 2-night Krabi base in Krabi Town.
  • Keep only light bags for this section; heavy suitcases remain in Bangkok.
  • Pre-arranged airport and late-night van transfers are important because local taxi availability can be weaker at night.

🌊 4-Island package — already included

  • Hotel pickup and drop-off
  • Professional guide
  • Private long-tail boat tour
  • Seasonal fruit + drinking water
  • Thai lunch
  • Snorkeling equipment

🚢 Dinner cruise

  • New Super Galaxy Dinner Cruise from ICONSIAM Pier 4.
  • Buffet dinner is included.
  • Allow the full 18:30–19:50 window for check-in and boarding readiness.

♿ Mobility guardrails

  • Grand Palace: use wheelchair assistance if prolonged standing becomes painful.
  • Jim Thompson House: steep wooden stairs and high thresholds.
  • Wat Arun: admire from the base; do not climb the steep central prang steps.
  • Long-tail boats: may require wading in shallow water and stepping over a high hull.
  • Tiger Cave: ground-level visit only; the summit staircase is completely excluded from this plan.

📱 Connectivity & group discipline

  • 2 Thai eSIMs are planned for the group.
  • Put one eSIM holder in each family so both families can contact drivers independently.
  • Use a fixed meet-up point at every dense market.
  • Keep passports and one payment method on the person, never in checked luggage or market shopping bags.
